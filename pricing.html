Common questions

Do I need to be registered as a business to sell?

Retailers can start with just a valid means of identification. Wholesalers and merchants need business registration details as part of verification.

How does the free plan make money for SuperKart?

Free plans carry a slightly higher commission on completed sales. As you upgrade, the commission drops and you unlock more tools.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your vendor dashboard, and changes apply from your next billing cycle.

When do I get paid?

Funds are held securely until an order is confirmed, then released to your wallet. Paid plans get faster settlement times.
