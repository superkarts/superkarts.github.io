What SuperKart handles for you

From your first listing to your hundredth bulk order, here's what runs behind the scenes.

Verified storefronts

Every vendor completes identity verification before their store goes live, using the same KYC checks ASBData already runs for its payments customers. Buyers can see a verification badge on every listing.

  • BVN/NIN-backed verification
  • Storefront builder with categories and variants
  • Custom store link

Marketplace discovery

Consumers search and filter stores by category and location, browse products, and check out without leaving the app.

  • Search and category filters
  • Ratings and reviews
  • One-tap sharing to WhatsApp and social

Connect & Quote (B2B trade)

Retailers request bulk quotes from wholesalers, and wholesalers request supply from merchants, directly inside SuperKart, replacing the informal calls and market visits this trade usually depends on.

  • Minimum order quantity tools
  • Bulk quote requests
  • Verified buyer and seller badges

Payments and escrow

Checkout runs on SuperKart Pay, built on ASBData's existing payment rails. Funds are held until an order is confirmed, protecting both the buyer and the seller.

  • Wallet, card, bank transfer, and USSD
  • Escrow-backed checkout
  • Fast payouts on paid plans

Delivery and logistics

Orders connect to third-party logistics partners at launch, with a dedicated SuperKart delivery option planned as the platform scales.

  • Order tracking
  • Delivery partner integration
  • Pickup option for local buyers

Advertising and growth tools

Vendors on paid plans can boost visibility with promoted listings and featured placement, and access sales analytics to see what's working.

  • Promoted listings
  • Featured homepage placement
  • Sales and buyer analytics

Mobile and desktop apps

Manage orders, inventory, and payouts from the SuperKart mobile app, with a desktop management dashboard for vendors running larger operations. Both unlock from the Growth tier upward.

  • Native iOS and Android app
  • Desktop dashboard for Growth tier and above
  • Multi-staff accounts on higher tiers
