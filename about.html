A marketplace for how Nigerian businesses actually trade

SuperKart started from a simple observation: the shop owner selling on WhatsApp, the wholesaler supplying five markets, and the distributor running a warehouse all need the same three things, a storefront, a way to reach buyers, and a safe way to get paid. Most platforms only build for one of them.

Photo: Tunde Buremo / Unsplash

SuperKart is built by ASBData to bring retailers, wholesalers, and merchants onto one platform, instead of treating consumer retail and business-to-business trade as separate problems. A retailer can discover a verified wholesaler and request a bulk quote without leaving the app. A merchant can supply wholesalers across the country through the same storefront tools a small shop owner uses to sell to their first customer.

Rather than building payments, identity verification, and wallets from scratch, SuperKart runs on infrastructure ASBData already operates for its existing customers. That means faster, more secure checkout from day one, and a platform that's accountable to the same standards ASBData holds its other products to.

What guides how we build

Every seller belongs

A market woman with twenty products and a distributor with twenty warehouses should both be able to trade online, at a price that fits their business.

Trust is built, not assumed

Every vendor is verified before they can list. Every payment is held until an order is confirmed. Trust is designed into the platform, not left to chance.

Built on what already works

SuperKart runs on ASBData's existing payments, wallet, and identity infrastructure, so it launches on a foundation that already serves real customers.

An ASBData company

SuperKart is developed and operated by ASBData. Vendor payments, verification, and wallet infrastructure are powered by ASBData's existing payments platform.
