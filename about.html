Our mission

Selling online shouldn't require a developer

Most small and medium businesses don't have the time, budget, or technical know-how to build a website, wire up payments, and figure out delivery — so they stay offline, or scattered across chats and social posts.

SMECart exists to close that gap: one place to open a store, get discovered in a shared marketplace, take payments safely, and manage orders — built around how small businesses actually operate.

What we believe

  • Every business, whatever size, deserves an online storefront.
  • Getting found shouldn't depend on an advertising budget.
  • Payments and delivery should be simple enough to trust.
